# Smart Medicine Reminder: User Instruction Manual

This guide is for patients and caregivers using the Smart Medicine Reminder system.

## 1) What this system does

- Reminds you on your phone at medicine time.
- Opens the correct medicine compartment on the box at schedule time.
- Lets you close/silence using device buttons.

## 2) Before first use

1. Charge/power the phone and the medicine box.
2. Install the Android app APK on your phone.
3. Turn on Bluetooth on your phone.
4. Open the app and allow:
   - Bluetooth permission
   - Notification permission
   - Exact alarms (important for accurate reminder time)

## 3) First-time setup (important)

1. Open the app.
2. Go to `Connect` tab.
3. Scan and connect to `Smart-Medicine-Reminder`.
4. Go to `Schedule` tab.
5. Set time for:
   - Medicine 1
   - Medicine 2
   - Medicine 3
6. Tap `SYNC / SAVE TO DEVICE`.

Result:
- Phone reminders are scheduled.
- Device clock and medicine schedule are saved.

## 4) Daily use

At medicine time:
1. Phone notification appears.
2. The correct compartment opens.
3. Buzzer sounds in beep pattern.

After taking medicine, use either method:
1. Press `Button A` on the device (recommended).
2. Tap the check button in the app.

## 5) Device buttons

## Button A (Acknowledge)

- Silences buzzer.
- Closes all compartments.
- Marks only currently due medicine as taken.

## Button B (Refill Mode Toggle)

- Before using refill mode, plug the device into the USB Type-C port at the back.
- Long press for about 2 seconds to toggle refill mode.

Refill mode ON:
- All compartments open and stay open.
- Use this when refilling medicines.

Refill mode OFF:
- All compartments close and normal operation resumes.

## 6) LCD messages and meaning

- `Medicine Box` / `Starting...`
  - Device is booting.

- `Time 10:26:45`
  - Current time display.

- `Next M2 3:35PM`
  - Next compartment and next medicine time.

- `Time for Med 1` / `Press A to close`
  - Medicine 1 is due now. Take medicine, then press Button A.

- `Time for meds` / `Press A to close`
  - More than one medicine is due now.

- `Refill Mode ON` / `Hold B to close`
  - Refill mode is active. Long-press Button B again to exit.

- `Clock not ready` / `Call caregiver`
  - Device time is not ready. Reconnect app and tap `SYNC`.

- `Set times in app` / `Ask for help`
  - Schedule is not configured yet. Set medicine times and tap `SYNC`.

## 7) App tabs quick guide

- `Schedule`
  - Set medicine times and sync.
  - Mark medicine as taken (check button).

- `Connect`
  - Scan and connect to device.

- `Records`
  - View daily intake summary.

- `About`
  - Project/about page.

## 8) If reminder does not appear

1. Check phone notification permission.
2. Check Exact Alarms permission.
3. Disable battery optimization for the app if your phone is aggressive.
4. Open app and tap `SYNC` again.

## 9) If device does not open compartment

1. Ensure box has stable power.
2. Ensure schedule was synced from app.
3. Check LCD for:
   - `Clock not ready`
   - `Set times in app`
4. Reconnect app and tap `SYNC`.

## 10) Safe-use reminders

- Keep medicines dry and out of reach of children.
- Refill on time.
- If a dose is uncertain, ask caregiver/doctor before taking extra medicine.
